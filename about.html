Engineer by degree,
designer by obsession.

The long version of how a first-year CSE student at JSSATE, Bangalore ended up chasing pixels instead of just circuits.

SP

I'm SaiPrateeth P, a first-year Computer Science Engineering student at JSS Academy of Technical Education (JSSATE), Bangalore. Somewhere between coursework and pulling apart apps I admire, I realised the part I actually love is the front end — the layer where a product finally becomes something someone can feel.

So I'm working backwards from engineer to designer: rebuilding interfaces I like, breaking them down, and asking why every pixel and transition sits where it does. I'm currently strong in HTML, CSS, SQL, Python, and C++, solidly comfortable with JavaScript, and just getting started with React — my aspiration is to become a frontend designer who ships interfaces that are both engineered well and designed with intent.

I'm based in Bangalore, India, learning by building — one project at a time — and open to internships and frontend design opportunities.
