About

Engineer by degree,
designer by obsession.

The long version of how a JSSATEB engineering student ended up chasing pixels instead of just circuits.

I'm SaiPrateeth P, currently pursuing engineering at JSSATEB. Somewhere between debugging assignments and pulling apart apps I admire, I realised the part I actually love is the front end — the layer where a product finally becomes something someone can feel.

So I'm working backwards from engineer to designer: rebuilding interfaces I like, breaking them down, and asking why every pixel and transition sits where it does. My aspiration is simple — become a frontend designer who can ship interfaces that are both engineered well and designed with intent.

Right now that means shipping small projects often, clone by clone, site by site, and getting a little more deliberate with every one.
